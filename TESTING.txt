NameplateSCT-Vanilla v0.4.2a

TESTING.md is the canonical test matrix for the current build.

Quick native-backend smoke test:
1. Enable enemy nameplates.
2. Target a visible enemy.
3. /np status
4. /np test
5. /np crit
6. Move out of nameplate range and back in.
7. /np errors
8. Confirm no immediate "plate disappeared" after /np test or /np crit.
9. /np clear (or /np clearlog) before a fresh capture.
10. If anything looks wrong: /np dump 50

v0.4.2a focus:
- native WorldFrame nameplate detection without requiring GUID APIs
- OnShow / OnHide lifecycle tracking
- native name indexing and target resolution
- optional exact GUID resolution when available
- no guessing for ambiguous same-named mobs
- native-resolution texts remain attached even when an auxiliary GUID exists
- independent capability reporting in /np status
- /np clear and /np clearlog diagnostics reset
- existing fountain/critical movement intentionally unchanged
